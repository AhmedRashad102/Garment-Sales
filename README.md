# Garment Sales & Business Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project is a Business Intelligence solution developed to analyze garment sales and operational performance using Microsoft Power BI.

The project evaluates three years of customer purchase order data alongside style-level sales data and six months of production performance data. It also examines customer FOB dates against actual FOB dates to assess delivery performance.

The objective is to transform raw business data into actionable insights that support management decisions across sales, customer performance, product styles, production efficiency, delivery reliability, and business risk.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze customer contribution to total turnover and sales volume.
- Evaluate year-over-year (YoY) performance across customers, product categories, and styles.
- Identify top-performing, growing, and declining garment styles.
- Monitor average FOB trends and potential commercial risks.
- Measure production achievement against planned targets.
- Evaluate on-time delivery performance and identify delays.
- Monitor customer, production, delivery, and commercial risk indicators.
- Support proactive decision-making through performance monitoring and risk analysis.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboards, KPI monitoring, business performance analysis, and data visualization.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Supporting data review and preparation, where applicable.
- **DAX:** Measures and KPI calculations within the Power BI data model, where implemented.

## Data Overview

The project is based on four main data components:

- **Customer Purchase Orders:** Three years of historical customer order data.
- **Style-Level Order Details:** Garment style, product category, turnover, quantity, and FOB information.
- **Production Performance:** Six months of planned and actual production data across production units.
- **FOB and Delivery Dates:** Customer FOB dates and actual FOB dates used to evaluate delivery performance.

## Power BI Dashboard Pages

The report contains nine pages organized into executive reporting, detailed performance analysis, and risk monitoring.

### 1. Summary
Provides an overview of business performance, including sales trends, customer indicators, production achievement, and delivery performance.

### 2. Customer Analysis
Evaluates customer turnover and volume, compares annual performance, and highlights differences in customer contribution and growth.

### 3. Customer & Category Analysis
Examines turnover and volume across product categories, styles, and countries, with year-over-year comparisons.

### 4. Style Performance
Analyzes garment styles by turnover and volume, compares performance across years, and identifies important contributors to sales.

### 5. Style & FOB Analysis
Evaluates average FOB by style and customer, tracks annual FOB changes, and supports the identification of potential pricing and commercial risks.

### 6. Production Performance
Compares planned and actual production, monitors achievement percentages and production gaps, and evaluates production units over time.

### 7. Delivery Performance
Tracks OTD percentage, average delay, late orders, and on-time orders to evaluate delivery reliability across customers and production units.

### 8. Risk Dashboard
Combines customer sales performance, delivery indicators, production gaps, and style-level FOB information to highlight potential areas of business risk.

### 9. Warning & Performance Monitoring
Provides detailed comparisons of customer turnover, order volume, FOB changes, and production and delivery performance to support the identification of emerging issues.

## Risk Monitoring

The analysis addresses four major risk categories:

- **Customer Risks:** Declining turnover, reduced order volume, and customer dependency.
- **Production Risks:** Low achievement percentages and gaps between planned and actual production.
- **Delivery Risks:** Low on-time delivery performance and increasing average delays.
- **Commercial Risks:** Declining FOB values and potential pricing pressure.
